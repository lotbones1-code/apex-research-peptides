Research peptides are used in scientific and academic laboratory research settings. They are utilized to study various biological processes, receptor interactions, and cellular mechanisms. All of our peptides are verified at 99% purity or higher via HPLC (High Performance Liquid Chromatography) analysis. Each batch undergoes independent third-party testing, and the results are documented in the Certificate of Analysis (COA) that accompanies every order.
A Certificate of Analysis is a document from an independent testing laboratory that verifies the identity, purity, and composition of a specific batch. Our COAs include HPLC purity data, mass spectrometry confirmation, and appearance/solubility testing. A COA is included with every order at no additional charge.

We accept major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, Ethereum), and ACH bank transfers. All transactions are processed through PCI-compliant payment processors with 256-bit SSL encryption for your security.
